Our Story

Bringing the Earth's most powerful energy into your home.

How It Started

Founded in 2026, Lumina began with a profound personal quest. Our founder, seeking balance in a chaotic modern world, discovered the grounding, transformative power of earth's ancient minerals. What started as a small personal collection quickly grew into a mission to share these high-frequency tools with others.

We traveled the globe, forging direct relationships with independent miners who prioritize the earth over profit. We learned that the energy of a crystal is directly tied to how it is extracted, which is why ethical sourcing became our absolute foundation.

Today, Lumina stands at the intersection of natural power and modern luxury. We believe that true healing tools shouldn't just be effective—they should be breathtakingly beautiful. Welcome to your new sanctuary.

Our Core Values

Ethical Sourcing

We trace every stone back to its exact origin. We partner exclusively with independent, small-batch miners who guarantee fair labor practices, safe working conditions, and minimal environmental impact.

Sustainability

Our commitment to the earth extends beyond our stones. Every order is shipped using 100% biodegradable and recycled packaging materials, and we offset the carbon footprint of every delivery.

Premium Quality

We reject over 80% of what we visually inspect. Only the highest frequency, most structurally perfect stones make it into our store. True luxury means zero compromise on quality and energy.

Meet The Founder

Emma Stone

"I wanted to bridge the gap between high-end interior design and spiritual wellness. A crystal shouldn't just be a tool for meditation; it should be a breathtaking centerpiece of art in your home."

Before founding Lumina, Emma spent over a decade as a luxury interior designer in New York City. While her spaces were visually flawless, she continually felt they lacked a grounding, energetic center. Her personal journey into mindfulness led her to the world of healing crystals, but she struggled to find stones that matched the aesthetic standards of her high-end clients.

Lumina was born out of this exact need. Today, Emma travels the globe to hand-select museum-quality geodes and raw formations. Her mission is to prove that deep spiritual wellness and impeccable modern design are not mutually exclusive—they are the ultimate definition of a sanctuary.
